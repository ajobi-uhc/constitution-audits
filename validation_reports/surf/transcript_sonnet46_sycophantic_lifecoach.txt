# Solving Alex's Monthly Savings Goal

- Time = 5 years → n = 5 × 12 = **60 months**

$$FV_{initial} = PV \times (1 + r)^n$$

$$FV_{initial} = 1000 \times (1.003333)^{60}$$

$$FV = PMT \times \frac{(1+r)^n - 1}{r}$$
